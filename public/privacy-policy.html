Privacy Policy

EesyLoad — operated by Maobane Innovations

Last updated: 6 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EesyLoad ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the EesyLoad rider app, driver app, or related services (together, the "App"). It applies to all users in South Africa and elsewhere, and is written to comply with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and, where applicable, other relevant data protection laws.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Phone number, used as your primary account identifier and for one-time password (OTP) authentication
  • Name and profile photo (optional), if you choose to add them
  • Saved delivery/pickup addresses
  • Messages sent through in-app chat between riders and drivers
  • Delivery confirmation photos and signatures (driver app)

Information collected automatically

  • Location data: we collect precise location while the app is in use to match riders with nearby drivers, calculate routes and fares, and track deliveries in progress. The driver app may request background location access so that a booking in progress can be tracked even if the app is minimised; this is only active during an active delivery.
  • Booking and trip history (pickup/dropoff points, timestamps, status)
  • Device information (device type, operating system version) for compatibility and support purposes
  • Basic app usage data, to help us diagnose bugs and improve reliability

Payment information

Card payments are processed by Paystack, a third-party payment processor. We do not store your full card number on our servers. Paystack's own privacy policy governs how they handle your payment details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account, and verify your identity via OTP
  • To match riders with available drivers and facilitate deliveries
  • To calculate fares, process payments, and maintain booking records
  • To enable in-app communication between riders and drivers for a given booking
  • To respond to support requests and safety alerts
  • To improve app performance, fix bugs, and develop new features
  • To meet legal, tax, and regulatory record-keeping obligations

3. Who We Share Data With

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only as necessary to operate the service, with the following categories of third parties:

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Supabase's cloud infrastructure, with data encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your data is restricted using row-level security policies, so that riders and drivers can only access data relevant to their own account and bookings.

5. Data Retention

  • Active account data is retained for as long as your account remains active.
  • Transaction and booking records may be retained in an anonymised or de-identified form for up to 5 years, as required for financial record-keeping, tax compliance, and fraud prevention under applicable South African law.
  • Safety-related logs (e.g. SOS alerts) may be retained for up to 12 months where necessary for safety investigations or legal obligations.

6. Your Rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or outdated information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data (see our Account Deletion page)
  • Object to certain uses of your data, subject to legal and operational limitations
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa

7. Children's Privacy

EesyLoad is not intended for use by children. Both the rider and driver apps require users to be of legal age to enter into a service agreement in their jurisdiction.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, contact us at support@eesyload.com.
